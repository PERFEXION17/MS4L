Privacy Policy

At My Store For Life, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you share with us when you visit our website, place an order, or subscribe to our newsletters.

Information We Collect

  • For Newsletter Sign-Up:

    • Email address

  • For Order Processing:

    • Full name
    • Phone number
    • Email address
    • Delivery address
    • Order details (including product, size, color, and quantity)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes

  • Order Fulfillment: To process and deliver your purchases, and provide order-related updates.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries or resolve issues regarding your orders.
  • Marketing & Newsletters: To send promotional emails, new arrivals, offers, and updates (only if you subscribe).
  • Service Improvements: To analyze trends and improve your shopping experience on our website.

Sharing of Information

We respect your privacy and will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors (e.g., payment processors, delivery services) strictly for order fulfillment.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to comply with legal processes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or loss.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Opt out of receiving marketing emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and provide tailored content. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.

Retention of Data

  • Newsletter email addresses will be retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Order-related details will be stored only as long as necessary to comply with legal, accounting, or service requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
