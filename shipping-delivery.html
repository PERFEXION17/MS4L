Shipping & Delivery

At My Store For Life, we are committed to getting your orders to you quickly and conveniently.

Order Dispatch

  • All items are available and dispatched immediately after payment confirmation.
  • Same-day dispatch may be available for orders placed before the cut-off time.

Delivery Days & Hours

  • Deliveries are made Monday to Saturday, from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
  • Customers are encouraged to be available during these hours to receive their packages.

Delivery Timeline

  • Local Deliveries (within Nigeria): 2-5 business days depending on your location.
  • International Deliveries: 7-21 business days depending on the destination and courier service.

Delivery Partners

  • We work with trusted courier and logistics companies to ensure safe and timely delivery.
  • Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your location.

Tracking Your Order

  • Once shipped, tracking details will be shared via email, WhatsApp, or SMS (where available).

Delays

  • While we strive for timely delivery, delays may occur due to courier issues, weather, or unforeseen circumstances. We will notify you if this happens.

Failed Deliveries

  • If delivery is unsuccessful due to an incorrect address or the recipient being unavailable during delivery hours, a re-delivery fee may apply.
