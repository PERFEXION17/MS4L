Meet the CEO — Felicia Onyemowo Onaji

Hi, I'm Felicia Onyemowo Onaji, the founder and CEO of My Store for Life.
I'm a Nigerian entrepreneur, content creator, and personal shopper with a passion for helping people feel confident, stylish, and seen in every space they show up. I currently balance building this brand while studying Veterinary Medicine full-time, and I proudly say: I'm doing both with heart.
My Store for Life is a curated space for women and men to shop fabrics, intimate clothing, lingerie, loungewear, shapewear, and more pieces that feel just as good as they look. From sourcing luxury items to styling everyday essentials, I love helping people find what fits, not just in size, but in vibe. Every product I stock or recommend is handpicked with intention, because I understand the value of quality, comfort, and soft confidence.
So whether you're here to shop, explore, or connect, Welcome. This brand was built for people like you: growing, glowing, and owning their space.
With love, Felicia Onyemowo Onaji Founder & CEO, My Store for Life.

At My Store For Life, we take pride in delivering high-quality intimate wear and fabrics. To maintain hygiene standards and ensure fairness, we have the following return and refund policy;


Returns & Exchanges

  • Items can be returned or exchanged within 30 days (1 month) from the date of purchase.
  • After 30 days, items are not eligible for return, refund, or exchange.
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with tags intact.

Non-Returnable Items

  • For hygiene reasons, intimate clothing (lingerie, bras, panties, shapers, nightwear, loungewear, etc.) cannot be returned once worn or used.
  • Sale or clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

Refunds (if applicable)

  • Approved refunds will be processed through the original method of payment.
  • Please allow up to 7-10 business days for the refund to reflect in your account.

Damaged or Wrong Items

  • If you receive a defective or incorrect item, please contact our customer care within 7 days of delivery for assistance.
